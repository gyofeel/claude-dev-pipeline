## Overview

Three screen checks for the sample app: the home title, the item list rendered from a mocked items API, and the link from home to the list.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-01**: The home page shows the title "Sample app" with no console errors.
- [ ] **AC-02**: The item list shows one entry per item from the items API, labelled with its `display_name`.
- [ ] **AC-03**: Selecting the "Items" link on the home page lands on `/list.html` and shows the items.
